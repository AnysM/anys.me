An international cross-disciplinary artist residency in the Moroccan Sahara.

Something rare happens when you step away from the noise and let the desert meet you. Desert Mirage Journey is an invitation to do exactly that, to feel the silence and the scale of the Sahara, and to create from what that space opens in you.

The theme this year is 間 Ma, the space in between. The interval where something can breathe and take form. Over twelve days we live nomadically in the desert, meeting its immensity and our own, immersed in a nomadic culture that carries ancestral wisdom.

Whether you arrive with a project, a question, or simply a creative impulse, you are welcome. Dancers, performers, musicians, visual artists, writers and seekers have all found their place here.

The residency is led by Yi Chen and Anys, who have guided initiatic desert journeys over several years. This year they open the experience to cross-disciplinary co-creation, where artists of every medium meet and create together.

Good to know
- Where : Moroccan Sahara, near M'Hamid el Ghizlane
- When : 10 to 21 December 2026
- Format : 12 days, cross-disciplinary, held in English
- Group : up to 14 participants
- Early Bird : available until 10 October
- Scholarships : available to support those who need them

All the details and applications are waiting for you at desertmiragejourney.com.
